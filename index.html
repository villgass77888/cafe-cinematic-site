The Story

Born from
obsession.

2022 — The First Roast

A small room in Bandra. A second-hand Probat. Three single-origins and an obsession with getting the pour just right. Aura started the way all good things do — with too much caffeine and not enough sleep.

2023 — Colaba Opens

The ritual found its second home in the old quarter. Exposed stone, low light, and the hum of the grinder at dawn. Colaba became the space where the ceremony took shape.

2024 — Origin Partnerships

Direct sourcing from Chikmagalur, Araku Valley, and the Nilgiris. Every bean traceable, every roast profiled by hand. No blends. No shortcuts. Just the craft.

2025 — Micro-Lots & The Lab

Exclusive micro-lots and a dedicated sensory lab for roast profiling. A playground for the palate where every variable is measured, tested, and perfected.

The Future — Unchanged Obsession

Our obsession remains unchanged. As we look forward, the ceremony continues. One pour, one cup, one moment of pause in a world that never stops moving.
