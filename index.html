The Story

Born from
obsession.

2018

One espresso machine. A single-origin Ethiopia. A refusal to compromise on a single cup.

2021

First roastery in Bandra. Still obsessed. Still making every cup like it's the only one.

2024

Two spaces. One philosophy. The same cup, every time, for everyone who walks through our door.

We started because we couldn't find the cup we were looking for. So we built the place that makes it.
